Initium Digital Library — Plan

13 October 2026 · Paper Analyzer team

Initium Library puts every teaching file and every past-paper question in one place, searchable across boards and levels. Our existing Paper Analyzer becomes its question engine. It is built in 5 phases over about 3 weeks, and each phase is usable on its own.

3. How content flows

Every upload goes through one door; the AI sorts it, an admin confirms, and it becomes searchable.

UploadTeacher or admin drops one or many files.
AI sortsReads the first page: type, board, level, subject, year, folder.
Admin confirmsOne click to accept, or change the folder.
SavedGoes to its folder; past papers go on to step 5.
QuestionsPaper Analyzer splits QP + MS into questions, answers, topics, crops.
Review & publishAdmin checks, fixes, publishes.
FindTeachers browse folders or Ask AI.
WorksheetPick questions → PDF → saved to Worksheets.
Notes, textbook, syllabus → stored and searchable by name, tags and text; no question splitting.
Past paper (QP + MS) → full Paper Analyzer pipeline, then appears in Question Bank and Ask AI.

4. Ask AI search

A teacher types a sentence; it becomes topic + level + difficulty and runs on what we already store, so most searches cost nothing.

Example: “projectile motion questions for AS, medium”

  1. Understand: common topic = Projectile motion, level = AS Level, difficulty = Medium (a small AI call, about $0.01 or less).
  2. Match across boards through the common topics: Edexcel 8PH0 “2.5 Projectile motion”, A Level Maths “7.5 Projectiles”, Cambridge 9702 kinematics.
  3. Add files whose names, tags or text match (notes, worksheets).
  4. Show results grouped by board and level, with “Add to worksheet”.
  5. Refine by asking again: “only 2020 onwards”, “make it 30 marks”.

9. Decisions needed from Initium

  • Who uses it: teachers only, or students too (published items only)?
  • IAL units such as Maths M1: show under “International A Level”, or also under “AS Level”?
  • Start with Phase 1 (folders and files)?
